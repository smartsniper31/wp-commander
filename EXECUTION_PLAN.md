## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Timeline Slippage
**Probability**: Medium  
**Impact**: Product launch delayed 1-2 weeks

**Mitigation**:
- Daily standups (catch issues early)
- 2-day buffer on each week
- Parallel workstreams (design + dev)
- Pre-planned escalation path

---

### Risk 2: Scope Creep
**Probability**: High (common in software)  
**Impact**: Delays, burnout

**Mitigation**:
- Strict Phase 0 scope lock (no new features)
- Feature requests go to Phase 4 backlog
- Weekly scope review

---

### Risk 3: Resource Unavailability
**Probability**: Medium  
**Impact**: Delays, quality reduction

**Mitigation**:
- Cross-training (backup for each role)
- Clear handoff documentation
- External contractor backfill planned

---

### Risk 4: Security Issues Discovery
**Probability**: Medium (crypto audit phase)  
**Impact**: Unplanned work, delays

**Mitigation**:
- 2-week buffer for security fixes
- Documented risk acceptance process
- External security consultant on standby
